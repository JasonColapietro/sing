# Fifth fleet pass: funnel, reading, rooms, imagery and bloat

Date: October 29, 2026. Baseline: `ef801f4` on `origin/main` (after #263).
Branch: `fleet-pass-5`.

Five read-only audits ran in parallel (conversion funnel and Pro asks,
reading surfaces, practice rooms, visual and imagery with full-page
screenshots of 25 routes at 375 and 1280, code bloat and CI), then fix lanes
with disjoint file sets. The full reports are in `fleet-pass-5/`:
`audit-funnel.md`, `audit-reading-copy.md`, `audit-rooms.md`,
`audit-visual.md` and `audit-bloat.md`.

## Verdict

Pass 4 mostly landed. Main was green (tsc, lint, 3,503 tests in 59 s), the
tree shrank 9.2 % to 20.4 MB, the homepage lost 1,900 px on a phone and its
drawn figures went from 5 to 18, and the hero, the 4.0 announcements, /pro's
"pay once" repetition and the dead room variants are fixed. What is left is
consistency rather than absence: a phrase pass 4 removed from the sales pages
("first two steps") still follows every free warmup and reaches Stripe
Checkout; song pages state the same five facts three times; the step runner
unified the phase machine but not its labels or helpers; mic-gate figures sit
in three positions at three sizes; and CI's wall time did not improve
(18.2 to 18.7 min) because one layout-shift shard ran 820 s against 155 s.

## Findings by audit (P1 and the bugs; the reports list P2 and P3)

### Conversion funnel and Pro asks
- "First two steps" survives on every free warmup result
  (`warmups/session-summary.tsx`, `routine-summary.tsx`) and in the Stripe
  product description, the last copy a buyer reads.
- UpgradeCard prints the price twice, side by side.
- The homepage Pro teaser says each perk twice and its top benefit once.
- Pass-4 status: hero, 4.0, "pay once" and cap wall fixed; /pro still lists
  the perks six times; six range-test CTA labels remain.

### Reading surfaces
- Song pages state title, key, range, span and rating three times, and the
  octave rule twice.
- The book's passaggio table, the atlas records "tables" and the voice-type
  roster are bullet lists: `lib/markdown.tsx` still cannot render a table or
  figure (a markdown extension is proposed in the report).
- Free book chapters say their "Try this" twice, chapter 8's buttons four
  times; the book repeats one safety sentence verbatim in 20 of 23 chapters.

### Practice rooms
- Bug (P1): "Pick a song first" puts a newcomer into an unscored listen run
  with no mic ask.
- Bugs (P2): the studio's "Sing again adds to the time" then discards it; the
  post-purchase "Sing your first pro warmup" link lands nowhere behind the
  newcomer fold; the X inside a routine step does three different things.
- A fresh /progress shows four dead-text empty states and three range-test
  asks. Pro asks still stack three to five deep on the range result and the
  recorder.

### Visual and imagery
- The hairline section label reached only the homepage and /pro; rooms still
  stack 6-10 boxed pills.
- Gate figures sit below the gate on /range, above it on /studio and inside it
  on /songs; phones lose the reference figures (`hidden sm:block`).
- Text walls remain on /warmups, /ear-training, /programs, /tools, /learn,
  the chapter readers and /changelog; three share cards still use a generic
  sans. The report ranks ten image additions and ten aesthetic fixes.

### Code, CI and bloat
- CI: the layout-shift shards were split by command count, 820 s against
  155 s, because the two full-route phone passes (240 s each) sat in one shard.
- `content/pdfs` is 27 % of the tree and #263 rewrote it again (+5.6 MB of
  history per re-render).
- `scripts/deploy-prod.sh` lost its clean-tree and `origin/main` guard while
  moving to `deploy --prebuilt`, which ships whatever is checked out.
- Duplication: the test hooks harness ×4, `openPath` ×3, `--executable=`
  parsing ×15, black-key sets ×10, `TONE_TEXT` ×3, routine helpers ×3, icons.
- Dead: `lib/singer-search-priority.ts`, three unreferenced `public/shot-*.png`
  (270 KB), a duplicate `compile-lessons --check` spawn, about 15 exports used
  only in their own file; `server-only` imported by 7 files but undeclared.

## Decisions

- The footer's credentials line stays (owner's request).
- The founder portrait stays in the hero.
- The PDFs stay committed this pass; moving them to Blob or LFS touches
  `/api/book/pdf` and `outputFileTracingIncludes` and is its own change.
- The layout-shift shards are split by measured time: `layout-shift` (every
  route as a newcomer at 375, then the capped, program, program-capped and
  saved-range states) and `layout-shift-returning` (the returning pass, the
  tall /singers window, the desktop deep links, rest day and the six
  finished programs), about 480 s and 500 s of suite time.
- `deploy-prod.sh` refuses a dirty tree or a `HEAD` other than `origin/main`.
