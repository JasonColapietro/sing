# Fourth fleet pass: copy, workflow, bloat and imagery

Date: October 28, 2026. Baseline: `384574e` on `origin/main` (after #261).
Branch: `fleet-pass-4`.

Six read-only audits ran in parallel (sales copy, practice-room workflow,
reading-surface copy, code bloat, developer workflow, visual and imagery with
full-page screenshots of 20 routes at 375 and 1280), then six fix lanes with
disjoint file sets. The owner's brief: find workflow and copy weaknesses and
redundant bloat; more images; a nicer aesthetic.

## Verdict

Main was green (tsc, lint, 3,454 tests). The identity is real but carried
almost entirely by type: outside the founder portrait and one coach-report SVG
the site had no photographs, figures or illustrations, and 19 of 20 rooms
opened on cream cards with no imagery above the fold. The copy repeats itself
inside pages rather than across them: the homepage said "live pitch feedback"
three times before the grid and announced 4.0 three times; /pro said "pay
once" nine times; a singer profile printed its answer twice and its caveat
seven times, on 636 pages. About half of the tracked bytes were generated or
reproducible output committed next to its source.

## Findings by lane (P1 only; the full reports list P2 and P3)

### Sales and landing copy
- Hero: seven text blocks and six links before the first scroll.
- 4.0 announced three times on the homepage (pill, violet card, footer).
- /pro: "pay once / never renews" nine times; the perks itemised five times.
- "Not just the first two steps the free plan shows" (an internal gating
  rule) sold as a benefit on five surfaces.
- Homepage sections a first visit does not need: the privacy strip repeats
  the hero and footer; comparison rows one and two are five "yes" marks.

### Practice-room workflow
- Ear: the recommended daily workout is 406 s against a 300 s free
  allowance, so a free newcomer's first ear workout always ends on the cap
  slide. Ear was the one room that did not size its pick to the allowance.
- Three hand-copied routine runners (warmups, ear, breath) with drifted
  labels: "Quit routine" / "Quit workout" / "Leave routine".
- About 950 lines of unreachable "page" variants still shipped, each with
  its own gate, start verb and a fourth Pro nudge.
- /songs: three to four taps and a taste quiz before the first note.
- Pro asks stacked three to six deep on single screens (12 surface types).

### Reading surfaces
- Singer profile: the subtitle and the "What is X's vocal range?" card were
  the same template; nine caveat sentences per page.
- Lesson pages: 24% of printed words were sentences shared by 50+ other
  lessons (step bodies and practice blocks were never filtered).
- Six wordings of the laryngologist paragraph across ~150 pages.
- Hubs said "most profiles await review" while 630 of 636 were confirmed.
- Singer prose committed three times (data JSON, singers-data, atlas-data)
  plus a fourth numeric copy; generated TS and PDFs were 39% of the tree.

### Bloat
- 22.4 MB tracked; ~3.1 MB hard bloat; 2.46 MB committed generated TS; 6.3 MB
  committed PDFs.
- Three DSP modules with zero non-test importers (1,902 lines with tests);
  52 unused exports; comments 19.8% of non-blank lines, with 117 lines of
  history-style comments and stale references.

### Developer workflow
- CI installed Chromium cold in five browser jobs (up to 582 s per job).
- Nothing ran on a push to main before Vercel deployed it.
- Three of four content compilers had no staleness guard.
- The deploy script built twice; two scripts nothing ran.

### Visual and imagery
- Homepage 44-48% text by pixel height; /singers 20k px on a phone; /atlas
  and /glossary 72-84% text.
- The iOS band's dark-green phone posters were the only off-palette element.
- Every room repeated the same template: kicker, serif h1, lede, cards,
  prose, FAQ.

## Decisions

- One answer, one caveat per page. The honesty stays; the dose drops.
- The footer is the crawl spine; the body stops duplicating it.
- Figures are drawn from the repo's own instruments (keyboard strips, pitch
  traces, range bars, waveforms) as server SVG in the coach-report style,
  labelled "Example". No stock photography. Product screenshots are rendered
  from the app itself.
- Paper gets a grain; panels stay smooth. In-body section labels lose the
  pill and gain a gold hairline. The line token steps up one notch so card
  edges exist on screen.
- Generated output stays committed this pass (zero-dependency build), but
  every compiler gains `--check` and a test runs them.
- PR #239 (book PDF) is superseded: the PDF is rebuilt from the current
  chapters with correct bookmarks.
