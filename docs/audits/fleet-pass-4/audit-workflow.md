# Practice-room workflow audit

Scope: the singer's path through /range, /studio, /warmups, /songs, /ear-training, /breath, /recorder, /programs, /progress, plus the shared practice primitives (components/practice/*, components/ui.tsx MicGate/EmptyState, components/audio/audio-setup.tsx, components/mic-alert.tsx, lib/free-cap.ts, lib/practice-limits.ts). Read-only; every claim below is tied to a file:line in /home/user/sing. Measured against PRODUCT.md principles 1 (obvious next action), 2 (legible feedback), 3 (consistent controls and terminology) and 5 (shared primitives first).

## Top findings

**P1 — The ear room's recommended workout always ends on the cap slide for a free newcomer** · `components/ear/ear-training-client.tsx:146-149`, `components/ear/routines.ts:83-89,189-195` · `recommendEarRoutine({ practicedToday })` returns `"daily"` for anyone who has not practised ear today, and the room renders it straight into the ContinueCard (`"Today's ear workout"`, `startLabel="Start workout"`, :238-251). Daily ear workout = higher-lower 60 s + interval 60 s + pitch-match 120 s + melody-echo 150 s + 4 × 4 s intros = **406 s**, against `FREE_DAILY_SEC = 300` (`lib/practice-limits.ts:2`). The cap is checked between steps (`ear-runner.tsx:410-413`), so a free singer's very first ear workout stops on `CapSlide` ("Free practice done for today") before step 4 and the results screen prints "not reached" rows. Warmups (`warmups-client.tsx:227 sizeRoutineToAllowance`) and breath (`breath-studio.tsx:222 sizeBreathRoutineToAllowance`) both size the recommendation to `cap.leftSec`; ear is the one room that does not. Quick ear (60+60+120+12 = 252 s) fits. **Fix:** add `sizeEarRoutineToAllowance(preferred, leftSec)` mirroring `components/warmups/routines.ts:291`, or better, one generic `sizeToAllowance(preferred, fallbackOrder, secondsOf, leftSec)` in `lib/free-cap.ts` used by all three rooms. **Effort: S.**

## What is already good

- **The shared practice layer is real and used everywhere it should be**: `SessionShell` (dark, focus-trapped, Escape), `ResultsScreen` (stars, XP, goal ring, rows, share, "See your tone"), `CountIn`, `ContinueCard`/`PathList`/`GoalRing`/`StreakChip` in `learn-home.tsx`, `step-results.ts`, and `free-cap.tsx`'s four cap surfaces. The ear, breath and warmup-routine rooms end on the same screen, which is exactly PRODUCT.md principle 5.
- **One privacy sentence** (`MIC_PRIVACY`, `ui.tsx:473`), **one mic-failure path** (`MicAlert`, scroll-into-view with tab-bar clearance), **one device picker** (`AudioSetup`), and one `FREE_DAILY_SEC` constant shared by server copy and the meter.
- **The cap is checked between steps, never mid-step** (`routine-runner.tsx:281-285`, `ear-runner.tsx:410-413`, `breath-runner.tsx:523-526`), and the results screen distinguishes "not reached" from "skipped". The warmups and breath picks are sized to the minutes left.
- **Results lead somewhere**: the range result's "What to do with this" card (`result-view.tsx:729-809`) hands off with `/warmups?exercise=<id>` and the songbook count; the pitch meter's `LastRun` card points at the range test or the next room; the warmups summary continues Today's three; songs chain through a setlist.
- **Deep links land on the thing named**: `/warmups?exercise=|?routine=&step=`, `/songs?song=`, `/breath?drill=|?routine=`, `/ear-training?routine=`, program rows resume at the first unsung step (`today-card.tsx:114-124`), and every one waits for the mic gate first.

- **Phone-first ordering is already fought for**: ContinueCard's `order-*` dance (`learn-home.tsx:324-391`), the gate buttons placed above the explanation on deep links (`warmups-client.tsx:686-703`, `mic-gate.tsx:100-112`), and the songs transport scroll (`songs-client.tsx:148-151`).
- **`ShareableResult` is one component for three rooms** and carries the room-specific star scale instead of growing copies.
