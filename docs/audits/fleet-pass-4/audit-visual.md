# Visual and imagery audit

Suede Sing, 2026-10-28. Production build (`npm run build` exit 0, 974 static pages) served with `next start -p 3100`; 20 routes captured at 375x812 and 1280x800, full page, as a first visitor with nothing dismissed. Read-only audit; nothing in `/home/user/sing` was changed.

## Verdict

The paper-and-serif identity is real and consistent, but it is carried almost entirely by typography: outside the homepage hero portrait and the one example coach report, the site has no photographs, no illustrations and no figures, so 19 of 20 rooms open on a wall of cream cards and the eye has nothing to grip until it reaches a live instrument it has not yet enabled. The homepage is 44-48% pure text by pixel height and over eight phone screens long before the footer; every room page repeats the same shape (kicker, serif h1, muted lede, bordered cards, long prose, FAQ), which is what makes them feel templated rather than like one family of rooms. The fix is not stock photography: the repo already contains the raw material for a dozen editorial figures (the keyboard strips, range bars, pitch trace, pro-visual, the social carousels' trace motif, the OG card generator), and three small token-level changes (texture, a second surface tone, a tighter prose measure) would lift every page at once.

## Image plan

Ranked by impact per unit of work. All buildable from the repo with no external licensing.

1. **Sample-data figures inside every mic gate (range, studio, songs, analyze, recorder, warmups).** Render the room's own instrument with example data above or beside the "Enable microphone" button, labelled "example". Range: `components/range/piano-strip.tsx` with marks at G2 and E4. Studio: a static `pitch-trace.tsx`-style SVG (eight-second lane grid, teal trace, green target band). Songs: a piano-roll figure (note blocks + trace). Analyze: paint a sample into the empty spectrogram canvas. Recorder: a sample waveform strip in the empty takes list. Warmups: a stepped five-note lane in "How a warmup works here". How: new `components/figures/*.tsx` server components drawing SVG with `lib/chart-colors.ts` constants, following `components/pro/pro-visual.tsx` (which already proves the pattern: viewBox SVG, mono labels, a "Sample data" chip in a panel2 header).
2. **Replace the iOS band posters with on-brand frames.** Crop the phone out of `shot-01/02/05` (they are 268x582, the phone occupies roughly the lower 75%) and present them inside a paper card with a thin line border and the caption, or better, render three 2x browser screenshots of Sing's own rooms with Playwright at 390x844 into `public/room-range.png`, `room-studio.png`, `room-progress.png` and show the phone app in a single smaller tile with its badge. How: `scripts/` already has Playwright; add a `scripts/render-room-shots.mjs` that seeds localStorage with a sample progress record (so the shots are not empty states) and saves `deviceScaleFactor: 2` PNGs.

## Aesthetic plan

Concrete token and primitive changes, ranked. All in `app/globals.css` or `components/ui.tsx` unless noted; each lifts every page.

1. **Paper texture on the ground.** `body` is a flat `#f7f0e7` with one radial amber glow at the top. Add a very fine grain so the "paper" claim is real: a second background layer of an inline SVG `feTurbulence` noise data-URI at 0.035 opacity (`background-image: url("data:image/svg+xml,…feTurbulence baseFrequency=0.8…"), radial-gradient(…), var(--color-bg)`), `background-size: 240px 240px` for the noise. ~600 bytes, no request, respects reduced motion (static). This is the single highest-leverage change: it separates panel (`#fffaf2`, left smooth) from ground and makes the cream read as material instead of a hex value.
2. **A second surface rhythm: alternate sections between bg and panel/40, and give one section per page a well.** The homepage already alternates `bg-panel/40`; the room pages never do, which is why 11k px scroll as one sheet. In `PageShell`, give the "short version / how to do it / questions" prose block (every room has it) a `well` or `bg-panel/40` band with `border-y border-line` and full-bleed width, so each page has visible movements: instrument, explanation, reference.
3. **Tighter prose measure and larger body in the reading sections.** Room prose runs at 16px across `max-w-2xl` / `max-w-prose`; the "short version" paragraphs on `/range` and `/studio` measure around 80-90 characters per line at 1280 and the glossary definitions run wider. Set a `.prose-room` rule: `max-width: 62ch; font-size: 1.0625rem; line-height: 1.65;` and apply it to the explanatory sections. Calmer reading, less grey wall.
4. **Serif at one more size tier, deliberately.** h1/h2 are the only serif; card h3 is Manrope 700. The big mono readouts on `/singers/adele` ("C3-A#5" at ~64px mono) and `/tools` ("96 BPM") are the most instrument-like type on the site. Add a `.readout` utility (`font-mono text-5xl sm:text-6xl tabular tracking-tight`) in `components/ui.tsx` (`Stat` with `size="hero"`) and use it for the one number each room produces (range span, cents, BPM, longest sustain, streak). One large number per page gives the eye the grip it lacks.
