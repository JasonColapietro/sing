# Reading surfaces copy audit

Scope: `/learn`, `/learn/voice/**` (1 + 7 stages + 34 modules + 102 lessons), `/book` (+23 chapters, 3 free), `/atlas` (+27 chapters, 3 free, + `vocal-range-by-voice-type`), `/singers` (+636 profiles, 29 genre hubs, 8 voice-type hubs, records, methodology), `/can-you-sing` (+26 songs), `/glossary`. About 900 pages. All line numbers are from the tree at commit `384574e`. Prose statistics come from a script over `content/**` (`/tmp/claude-0/-home-user/f42fa408-4574-5c8b-b322-b364a2b633e8/scratchpad/prose_stats.py`) and a re-implementation of the lesson page's own filter over `lib/lesson-data.ts` (`…/scratchpad/lesson_boilerplate.mjs`). Nothing in `/home/user/sing` was modified.

Headline numbers:

- 102 lesson pages: after the existing `UNIVERSAL_LESSON_TEXT` filter, **24% of the words printed on an average lesson page (123 of 507) are sentences that appear on 50+ other lesson pages**; the practice-blocks card is 96% template (5,333 of 5,577 words). With the safety note and the practice panel added, roughly 300 of ~700 words on a typical lesson page are shared boilerplate.
- 636 singer profiles: the sentence "not an independently verified physiological limit" prints **seven times per page**, and the page's subtitle and its "What is X's vocal range?" answer card are **verbatim identical** (same template at `app/singers/[slug]/page.tsx:151` and `:220`).
- Generated data: singer blurb/technique prose (351 KB) is committed **three times** (`data/singers/*.json` → `lib/singers-data.ts` → again inside `lib/atlas-data.ts`), plus a fourth numeric projection in `lib/singers-lite.ts`. 2.46 MB of generated TypeScript and 6.3 MB of generated PDFs are tracked: 39% of the 22.4 MB in HEAD.
