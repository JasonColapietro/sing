## Release: Suede Sing 3.1

The owner named the September wave **3.1**, skipping 3.0. It ships with the PR
that adds this file:

- **`/changelog`** now leads with six 3.1 stories: lessons, programs, songs,
  breath and vibrato measurement, the pitch engine, and daily practice. The v2
  stories stay underneath as "Earlier: v2, August 2026". The running log gains a
  September 2026 month with 21 entries, dated by merge day in US Eastern time.
- **The site-wide banner** (`components/v2-banner.tsx`) now announces 3.1 with
  the copy "102 voice lessons, multi-week programs, and songs that score you."
  `SEEN_KEY` went from `suede-sing:v2-banner:v1` to `suede-sing:v3-1-banner:v1`,
  so people who dismissed the v2 banner see it once more. The file, component
  and DOM id keep their v2 names, which are internal only.
- **The footer link** reads "3.1: what we've changed".

The owner asked for changelog copy that is **technical and never
unflattering**. Every figure in it was checked against the code or the PR that
shipped it:

- 7 stages, 34 modules and 102 lessons, from `contracts/suede-voice-curriculum.json`
- 6 programs, 4 free, with Pro programs' `freeWeeks: 1`, from `contracts/suede-vocal.json`
- vibrato bias from 5.5 c to 0.7 c (#152)
- tempo 25–125% in 5% steps (#129)
- inhale detection with a 2048-point FFT over 0.25–2.5 s (#178)
- a vibrato target band of 5–7 Hz (#171)
- Note Catcher's 0.4 s hold (#161)

Fixes, caps and caveats are left out of the public copy on purpose. When you
add entries, keep this style: one line each, what a singer can use, newest
first. Don't give numbers the code doesn't hold, and keep em dashes out of the
title and meta (#132).
