Why that happens
Every number the app shows you measures two things at once: your singing, and the conditions you sang in.

Usually the second is quiet enough to ignore. Sometimes it is louder than the first — and you end up reading the room instead of your voice.

02 / 07
Four things that corrupt a session
Room noise
A fan, a fridge, traffic. Low steady noise sits in the same neighbourhood as your low notes.
Mic distance
Too close and loud notes clip. Too far and your quietest notes never register at all.
Headphone bleed
The mic hears the backing track too, and your score drifts toward the reference tone.
The day itself
Sleep, talking, dryness, the hour. None of it is a fault. All of it moves the numbers.

The classic false reading
“
You sang the note, the app did not hear it, and the result says you cannot reach it.
04 / 07
The ten-second check
Open the pitch studio, stay silent, and watch.
Clean
Nothing. Silence reads as silence.
Noisy room
Phantom notes, with nobody singing.

If the trace finds notes in your silence, it will find them while you sing too, and mix them into the reading.

05 / 07
Tells that a session went wrong
Accuracy uniformly bad across your whole range — voices don’t degrade evenly, setups do.
Low notes erratic, middle clean.
Notes you sang last week that don’t appear at all.
Everything sharp or flat by the same amount on every note.

One question before a number changes your plan: was this session measurable?
