## Caption (paste-ready)

```
"Treat the two numbers like a bathroom scale. Real, useful across months, close to meaningless on any single reading."

A range test gives you two notes and a closest-fit label. The result becomes useful when you can reproduce the conditions that produced it.

Use the same short warmup. Test in the same room at roughly the same hour. Hold the phone a hand's span from your mouth. Count only a clear pitch you can hold for two beats. Stop when effort replaces height.

Cold voice, a different hour, pushing, room noise, and rushed edge notes can all shorten the reading. If two explanations fit, discard the result and test another day.

The label is a starting point for keys and repertoire. It does not measure your identity, potential, tessitura, or permission to sing a note.

Chapter eight of The Measured Voice is free to read. Link in bio.
```
