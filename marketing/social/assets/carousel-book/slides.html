What that means
Nothing about it is stored. There is no reed, no string, no fixed tube of brass — just moving air, a pair of small folds of tissue that get in its way, and a shaped space above them that colours whatever comes out.

“That is the whole machine.”

Three questions, not one

Hear something you don’t like in a recording, and the model gives you somewhere to look.

Breath?
Wobbling volume, notes that sag, running out of air.
Folds?
Breathiness, a crack at a predictable spot, a thin top.
Tract?
Dullness, a woolly vowel, a note two sizes smaller.
