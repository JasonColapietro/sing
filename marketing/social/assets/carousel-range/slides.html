The part most posts skip

Range and voice type
are different
measurements.

Range is the distance between your two extreme notes — often one-off moments.

Voice type is where your voice sits comfortably for a long time, plus its weight and colour.

A soprano with a practiced low extension can show a lower floor than a contralto who never sings down there. Neither label is wrong.
