## Public search indexing

Published public content is indexable by default. Never add `noindex` or omit a
published singer profile from the sitemap because an editorial/review-status
field is incomplete or stale. Keep source limitations truthful and reconcile
review records separately. All published singer profiles must pass the broad
metadata and sitemap indexing regressions, including a pending-status fixture.
Account/auth pages, private data, unpublished routes, and paid-content shells
retain their explicit indexing exclusions.
