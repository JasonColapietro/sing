# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Singers practicing at home with a phone or laptop, using a microphone for immediate feedback. New visitors need a direct path to hear a reference, sing a note, and understand the result. Returning singers need their routine, songs, and progress within easy reach.

## Product Purpose

Suede Sing provides browser-based pitch feedback, vocal range measurement, guided warmups, song practice, ear and breath training, and a voice curriculum. Success is a completed, understandable practice session and a clear next step. Audio analysis happens on the device.

## Brand Personality

Warm, precise, encouraging. The existing interface combines paper surfaces, editorial headings, and instrument-like measurements. Preserve the official Suede mark and the distinction between reading and practicing. Describe supported capabilities confidently and explain controls in plain language.

## Anti-references

Avoid the overwhelming exercise grid and all-controls-visible player documented in the warmup design brief. Avoid generic neon music dashboards, decorative glass panels, competing primary actions, and dense caption-sized instructions. Do not invent claims or change the offer during a visual pass.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next practice action obvious. Reveal advanced adjustments progressively.
2. Keep feedback immediate and legible while someone is singing at arm's length.
3. Preserve predictable controls and consistent terminology between rooms.
4. Keep reading surfaces calm, with comfortable line lengths and clear hierarchy.
5. Improve shared primitives before introducing screen-specific treatments.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain visible keyboard focus, labeled controls, meaningful landmarks, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support. Aim for 44px touch controls without hiding content or changing musical measurements. Test narrow phones, tablets, and desktop layouts. Use text and shape alongside state colors.
